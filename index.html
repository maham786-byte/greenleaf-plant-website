🌱 Discover Plants | Pauday

🪴

Money Plant

Water: Jab mitti ki upar wali satah sookh jaye.

Light: Bright, indirect light.

Isay roshni wali jagah rakhein.

🌵

Snake Plant

Water: Mitti sookhne par pani dein.

Light: Kam ya darmiyani roshni mein bhi reh sakta hai.

Zyada pani dene se bachayein.

🌱

Aloe Vera

Water: Pani dene se pehle mitti sookhne dein.

Light: Bright light.

Gamlay se extra pani nikalna chahiye.

🌹

Rose (Gulab)

Water: Mitti ki zaroorat ke mutabiq pani dein.

Light: Aam tor par kai ghantay dhoop.

Gulab ko achhi drainage wali mitti chahiye.

🌿

Tulsi (Basil)

Water: Mitti ko halki nami mein rakhein.

Light: Achhi dhoop.

Mitti mein pani jama na hone dein.

🌼

Peace Lily

Water: Jab upar ki mitti sookhne lage.

Light: Seedhi tez dhoop se bachayein.

Iski pattiyon ko check karte rahein.

🌻 Basic Plant Care Tips

💧 Pani dene se pehle mitti check karein.

☀️ Har plant ki roshni ki zaroorat alag hoti hai.

🪴 Pani nikalne wale holes ka gamlay use karein.

🌱 Sookhay pattay aur keeray check karein.
